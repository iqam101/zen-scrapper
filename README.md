# Zen Scrapper - GMB Lead Scraper & Outreach Engine (v2.0 Offline Edition)
**Agency:** Zenthoz Digital Marketing ([www.zenthoz.com](https://www.zenthoz.com) • +1 307 301 2270)  
**Author:** Iqam Ifthikar  
**Version:** 2.0.0  
**License:** Private / Commercial  

Zen Scrapper is a high-performance Google Chrome Extension (Manifest V3) designed for **Zenthoz Digital Marketing** to scrape high-ticket local service businesses directly from **Google My Business (Google Maps)**, audit website & security faults, run live automated **Deep Search** web research, classify businesses into verified trade niches, and generate ready-to-send cold outreach kits with zero cloud dependencies.

---

## 🚀 Key Features in Version 2.0.0

### 1. 🧽 Dedicated Car Wash & Auto Detailing Category (13 Niches)
- Comprehensive coverage of: Hand Car Wash & Valeting, Mobile Detailing Van, Auto Detailing & Paint Correction, Ceramic Coating & PPF, Automatic & Drive-Thru Wash, Touchless Wash, Commercial Fleet & Truck Wash, Self-Service Jet Wash, Interior Deep Clean, Window Tinting & Vehicle Wraps, Paintless Dent Repair (PDR), Alloy Wheel Refurbishment, and Engine Bay Wash.
- Auto-classified so detailing shops are never mixed up with general mechanic garages.

### 2. 🇪🇺 Built-In Global Directory (18 Countries & 86+ Niches)
- Pre-loaded with top markets:
  - 🇺🇸 **United States** (Texas, Florida, California, Wyoming, Georgia, Ohio, etc.)
  - 🇬🇧 **United Kingdom** (London, Buckinghamshire, West Midlands, Manchester, Yorkshire, Scotland, etc.)
  - 🇨🇦 **Canada** (Ontario, British Columbia, Alberta, Quebec)
  - 🇦🇺 **Australia** (NSW, Victoria, Queensland, WA)
  - 🇩🇪 **Germany**, 🇫🇷 **France**, 🇪🇸 **Spain**, 🇮🇹 **Italy**, 🇳🇱 **Netherlands**, 🇮🇪 **Ireland**, 🇨🇭 **Switzerland**, 🇧🇪 **Belgium**, 🇸🇪 **Sweden**, 🇳🇴 **Norway**, 🇩🇰 **Denmark**, 🇵🇱 **Poland**, 🇦🇹 **Austria**, 🇵🇹 **Portugal**.
- Instant query generator with 1-click **Launch on Google Maps**.

### 3. 🏷️ Universal Niche Classifier
- **Rating Mark Immunity**: Strips out score marks like `2.8(33)` or `4.9 (45)` so they never pollute categories.
- **Contextual Categorization**: Scans business titles and search queries to cleanly classify businesses into actual trades with live dropdown counts.

### 4. 🔍 Live Deep Search with Research Audit Logs
- Uncovers missing official websites, Facebook pages, Instagram, LinkedIn profiles, decision-makers, and direct emails.
- Transparent **Researched Sources & Web Audit Trail** log for every search.

### 5. ⚡ Outreach Kit Generator
- Instant **Cold Email** with personalized fault pitch and Zenthoz agency branding.
- **Cold Call Script** with objection handlers.
- **Social Media DM** for Instagram and Facebook.

### 6. 📇 Scoped Multi-Filter & Google Contacts Export
- Multi-filter by status (`🚨 No Website`, `⚠️ Website Faults`) and by specific niche.
- **Google Contacts CSV**: Direct 1-click import into [Google Contacts](https://contacts.google.com).
- Standard CSV export for Excel/CRMs with smart auto-naming.

### 7. 🔒 100% Offline & Private Local Storage
- Operates strictly inside Chrome's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`).
- Zero cloud servers, zero monthly subscriptions, zero sign-in walls.
- Built-in data hygiene: `🧹 Clear Leads`, `🗑️ Delete Campaign`, and `⚠️ Wipe All`.

## 🛠️ Quick Installation Guide

1. Open **Google Chrome** and go to `chrome://extensions`.
2. Toggle on **Developer mode** in the top-right corner.
3. Click **Load unpacked** in the top-left corner.
4. Select this directory (`lead-scraper-extension`).
5. Pin the Zen Scrapper extension icon to your browser bar.
