## 🚀 Quick 60-Second Installation

You do not need to compile or install any software. All you need is Google Chrome (or Edge / Brave).

### Step 1: Extract the Zip File
1. Download or locate `Zen_Scrapper_v2.0_Offline.zip`.
2. Right-click the file and select **Extract All...** (or unzip it).
3. Choose a convenient folder on your computer (e.g. your `Documents` or `Desktop`). You will have a folder named `Zen_Scrapper_Extension`.

### Step 2: Open Chrome Extensions
1. Open Google Chrome.
2. In the top URL bar, type:
   ```text
   chrome://extensions
   ```
   and press **Enter**.
3. In the top-right corner, **turn ON the "Developer mode"** toggle switch.

### Step 3: Load the Extension
1. In the top-left corner, click the **"Load unpacked"** button.
2. Browse to and select the extracted `Zen_Scrapper_Extension` folder (the folder containing `manifest.json`).
3. Click **Select Folder**.

### Step 4: Pin Zen Scrapper
1. Click the **Puzzle icon** (Extensions) in the top-right corner of Chrome's toolbar.
2. Find **Zen Scrapper** and click the **Pin (📌)** icon next to it so it stays visible on your toolbar.

🎉 **That's it! Zen Scrapper is installed and ready to use.**

## 🗺️ How to Scrape Leads (Step-by-Step)

### Option A: The Fast 1-Click Launch (Recommended)
1. Click the **Zen Scrapper icon** on your Chrome toolbar.
2. Click **"📊 Open Full Leads Dashboard"**.
3. In the top navigation bar, click the green button: **"🎯 Niches & Areas"**.
4. Use the **Fast Query Generator**:
   - **Select Country**: Pick from 18 built-in nations (USA, UK, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Netherlands, Switzerland, Canada, Australia, etc.).
   - **Select State / Region**: (e.g., *Bavaria*, *Texas*, *Île-de-France*, *Buckinghamshire*).
   - **Select City / Area**: (e.g., *Munich*, *Austin*, *Paris*, *Milton Keynes*).
   - **Select Category**: (e.g., 🧽 *Car wash and auto detailing*, 🏡 *Home exterior*, 🛋️ *Home interior*, 🚨 *Emergency & restoration*).
   - **Select Specific Niche**: (e.g., *Hand car wash & valeting*, *Mobile detailing*, *Roofing*, *Tree surgeon*).
5. Click **"🗺️ Launch on Google Maps"**.

---

### Option B: Scraping on Google Maps
1. Once Google Maps opens with your search results, you will see the floating **Zen Scrapper Controller Widget** in the top-right corner.
2. Click the green **"▶ Start Scraping"** button.
3. Zen Scrapper will automatically:
   - Smoothly auto-scroll through the Google Maps business listings.
   - Extract the business name, address, phone number, rating, reviews, website, and niche category.
   - Run a live technical audit on their website in the background.
4. When you have enough leads (e.g. 20, 50, 100+ leads), click **"⏹ Pause"** or **"📊 View in Dashboard"**.

## ⚡ Key Features & Capabilities

### 1. 🔍 Automated 14-Point Lead & Technical Website Audit
Every scraped lead is automatically checked across 14 data fields:
- **Business Details**: Business Name, Category, Physical Address, Direct Phone Number, Rating, and Review Count.
- **Decision-Maker / Owner**: Researches founder or head contact names.
- **Website & Security Fault Detection**:
  - ⚠️ **No SSL**: Identifies websites running on insecure `http://` where Chrome shows "Not Secure" warning to clients.
  - 📱 **Missing Mobile Viewport**: Detects websites that break or don't scale properly on smartphones.
  - ⏳ **Outdated Copyright**: Flags websites not updated since 2019 or older.
  - 📋 **No Online Quote Form**: Detects missing after-hours lead capture forms.
  - ❌ **No Independent Website**: Catches businesses that only use a Facebook page or free Google listing as their website.
- **Social Profiles**: Links to Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn.
- **Custom Pitch Angle**: Automatically writes a persuasive reason to reach out based on the business's exact weakness.

## 💡 Pro Tips for High-Converting Outreach

1. **Target the "No Website" Golden Nugget**:
   - Filter by **`🚨 No Website`**.
   - These are established businesses with 4.5+ star reviews and hundreds of customers, but zero website presence.
   - Offer them a modern, mobile-friendly 1-page booking site to stop losing local clients to competitors.
2. **Pitch the "Not Secure (No SSL)" Fix**:
   - Filter by **`⚠️ Website Faults`**.
   - Tell the business owner that when potential clients click their link in Chrome, they get a red "Your connection is not private" warning screen. Offering to fix their SSL certificate is the easiest foot-in-the-door service.
3. **Leverage Google Contacts**:
   - Export your filtered list as **Google Contacts CSV**.
   - Import it into [contacts.google.com](https://contacts.google.com).
   - If your phone is synced with Google, all business phone numbers will appear on your smartphone with their business names already saved, making cold calling seamless!
